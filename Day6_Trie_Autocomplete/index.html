What is a Trie? (The Core Concept) 💡

Imagine a dictionary where words are organized by their prefixes. Instead of looking at a word like "APPLE" as one single thing, a Trie breaks it down into steps: A → P → P → L → E.

How it works (In Simple Terms):

  • Sharing is Caring: If you have "APP" and "APPLE", the first 3 characters are shared. The tree only creates NEW nodes when a word branches off.
  • The Search: When you type 'A', the computer jumps straight to the 'A' branch. It doesn't look at words starting with 'B', 'C', or 'D' at all. This is why it's incredibly fast.
  • End of Word: We use a special marker (the purple glow in this app) to say "a complete word ends here".

Why use this?

Search engines like Google use Tries because they can find suggestions for your search out of billions of words in milliseconds.
